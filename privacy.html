Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

EgbertsAI Prompt Assistant is designed to help users create, improve, and adapt prompts for artificial intelligence tools. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how the service works.

1. Information We Collect

The EgbertsAI Prompt Assistant plugin is currently provided as a set of instructions and skills. It does not independently collect, store, or transmit your prompts, conversations, or personal information to an external database or server.

2. How Your Information Is Used

The plugin uses the information you provide within your AI platform to help generate or improve prompts. It does not operate its own user registration system, external analytics service, or independent database.

3. Third-Party AI Platforms

When you use this assistant through platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or other AI services, those platforms may process your prompts, conversations, and other information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

EgbertsAI does not control the privacy practices, data retention, or security policies of these third-party platforms.

4. Data Sharing

EgbertsAI does not independently sell, rent, or share personal information collected through this plugin because the plugin does not independently collect or maintain such information.

5. Data Security

We encourage users to avoid entering sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information into AI tools unless they understand and accept the relevant platform's privacy and security policies.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the assistant develops or its functionality changes. Updates will be published on this page.
